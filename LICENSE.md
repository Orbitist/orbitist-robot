## What this means in plain terms

- **Anyone may build, use, modify, and sell** robots made from these designs, including farmers, schools, and companies.
- **Hardware (weakly reciprocal):** if you distribute a modified version of *our* design files, or products made from them, you must share those modifications under the same license. You may combine the robot with your own separate modules, such as a proprietary arm or tool, without opening those up.
- **Software (permissive):** use it anywhere, including closed products. Keep the copyright and license notices. Apache-2.0 also includes an explicit patent grant. It matches the ROS 2 ecosystem and can be combined with GPLv3 ArduPilot.
- **Docs (share-alike):** reuse and adapt freely with attribution; adapted docs stay under CC BY-SA.

Third-party projects referenced here (ArduPilot, ROS 2, EFeru firmware, etc.) keep their own licenses.
