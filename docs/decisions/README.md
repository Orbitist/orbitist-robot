# Decision log

A short record of design decisions, so future contributors can see *why* things are the way they are. Add a row when a decision is made or revisited. Write a longer note only when a row isn't enough.

| # | Date | Decision | Status | Rationale / link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10-08 | Build **one robot first**; peer-to-peer docking becomes a later research track | Proposed | Mowing doesn't need a pair; the latch was the top risk. [platform-v1 §2](../design/platform-v1.md#2-what-changes-from-the-vineyard-concept-and-why) |
| D2 | 2026-10-08 | **Zero-turn layout** (2 rear hub motors + 2 front casters) for v1; frame accepts swappable drive modules (wheelchair, 4WD) | Proposed | Turf-friendly pivot turns; proven mower geometry. [§3](../design/platform-v1.md#3-chassis-options-considered) |
| D3 | 2026-10-08 | **ArduPilot Rover** on a flight controller for low-level control and GPS navigation; Pi 5 + ROS 2 added as a companion in phase 2 | Proposed | Mature skid-steer, RTK, missions, failsafes; existing community mower builds. [§6.3](../design/platform-v1.md#63-navigation-and-control) |
| D4 | 2026-10-08 | **RTK GNSS (u-blox ZED-F9P) in phase 1** | Proposed | Coverage mowing needs cm-level repeatability |
| D5 | 2026-10-08 | **VESC-class FOC controllers** by default; hoverboard + EFeru FOC as budget option | Proposed | Direct RC-PWM/CAN from ArduPilot; Hall-sensored low-speed torque |
| D6 | 2026-10-08 | **Mower is a belly-mount module**; v1 uses a salvaged 40 V-max cordless mower deck on its own battery | Proposed | Includes blade brake and height adjust; isolates blade power. [§6.2](../design/platform-v1.md#62-mower-module) |
| D7 | 2026-10-08 | Haul loads by **towing a cart from a rear hitch** rather than carrying them on deck | Proposed | Cart's wheels carry the load; tongue weight adds traction |
| D8 | 2026-10-08 | Battery swap first, then a **fixed AprilTag charging dock** in phase 2 (replaces peer-to-peer charging for lawn use) | Proposed | Simpler; ArduPilot Dock mode exists |
| D10 | 2026-10-08 | Operating model for ~3 acres: **mow in daily zones from a charging dock**; dock is the first item in phase 2 | Proposed | One 21" deck needs ~12.5 h per full pass at 0.6 m/s. [platform-v1 §5](../design/platform-v1.md#5-back-of-envelope-sizing) |
| D11 | 2026-10-08 | **Own RTK base station** (ZED-F9P, position fixed with NOAA OPUS), 915 MHz radio link to the robot; NTRIP caster later | Proposed | No known correction service nearby; one base serves the whole farm |
| D12 | 2026-10-08 | Mower module from a **second used Ryobi mower** sharing the farm's existing batteries and charger | Proposed | Keeps the daily mower in service; reuses the battery ecosystem. Pending model number. |
| D13 | 2026-10-08 | **Code-based CAD (build123d) in `hardware/cad/` is the source of truth**; Onshape/FreeCAD for viewing STEP exports and quick hand-sketched parts (revised same day; originally Onshape) | Accepted | Parametric, diffable in git, regenerates DXFs/cut list/strength check, editable by Claude. | D14 | 2026-10-08 | **Frame built for twin decks from day one** (~1.3–1.4 m wide); v1 runs one centered deck | Proposed | 3+ acres, flat, 8 ft paths; widening later would mean rebuilding the frame. [platform-v1 §4](../design/platform-v1.md#4-v1-layout-top-view) |
| D15 | 2026-10-08 | **Licenses:** CERN-OHL-W-2.0 (hardware), Apache-2.0 (software), CC BY-SA 4.0 (docs) | Accepted | Anyone can build and sell; changes to our hardware design are shared back; third-party modules can stay closed; software matches ROS 2. [LICENSE.md](../../LICENSE.md) |
| D16 | 2026-10-08 | From the strength check: **torque arms required**, **saddle-mounted drive forks**, **gussets** at the drive-adjacent joints, **30x60 cross member at x = 215**, **no towing** until an A-frame drawbar is added | Accepted | [strength-report.md](../../hardware/cad/exports/strength-report.md) |
| D17 | 2026-10-08 | **Plate-mount 10" pneumatic swivel casters** on 8 mm steel corner plates (instead of zero-turn mower casters); frame raised to 320 mm; front cross member 30x60 | Accepted | Bolt-on, no welded spindle housing, cheap and swappable. Upgrade to mower casters if shimmy is a problem. |
| D18 | 2026-10-08 | **Category 1 e-stop** (signal cut → VESC brake → contactor opens after 1 s), hardware-only path; **fail-safe wireless e-stop** via microcontroller watchdog; **v1 mowing speed 0.6 m/s** to stay within bumper stopping distance | Accepted | Hub motors coast if power is cut instantly; the bumper only protects ≤ 0.61 m/s. [electrical](../../hardware/electrical/README.md), [strength-report](../../hardware/cad/exports/strength-report.md#bumper-stopping-distance) |
| D19 | 2026-10-08 | Base position from a **24 h log processed by CSRS-PPP**; configure the F9P with our own script (no u-center, so it works on Mac and Pi) | Accepted | [rtk-base-station guide](../guides/rtk-base-station.md) |
| D20 | 2026-10-08 | **v1 deck in the rear-right slot**, not centred | Accepted | Cuts within ~19 cm of obstacles on the right (vs ~43 cm); planner laps put the deck on the boundary; the twin upgrade only adds a deck |
| D21 | 2026-10-08 | **Simulation gate:** every parameter or Lua change must pass `software/sim/run_sitl.py` before it goes on the robot | Accepted | SITL found eight real problems before hardware ([sim README](../../software/sim/README.md)) |
| D22 | 2026-10-08 | Autopilot settings from SITL: `AVOID_ENABLE 2` (no fence avoidance), `WP_RADIUS 0.2`, 15 cm pass overlap, 0.25 m extra obstacle clearance, **pause on RTK loss** (Lua) | Accepted | Each fixes a failure seen in simulation |
| D23 | 2026-10-08 | Own **coverage planner** (perimeter laps + stripes + visibility-graph routing, fences within 80 points) instead of Mission Planner's grid tool | Accepted | Knows the offset deck, the body width, and obstacle clearance; one mission + fence per zone |
| D24 | 2026-10-08 | **De-risk before buying** (T1 Ryobi blade power, T2 RTK survey, T3 one-motor bench test, T4 caster); **battery choice waits for T1** | Accepted | Blade power and motor heating are the two biggest unknowns ([confidence plan](../design/confidence-plan.md)) |
| D25 | 2026-10-08 | VESC PPM in **PID speed** mode (throttle ∝ wheel speed, ~2 m/s full scale) | Proposed | 0.6 m/s = 30 % throttle instead of ~7 % duty; finer low-speed control |
| D26 | 2026-10-08 | The platform is a *system*: v1 carrier for mass + mowing, a 1-D bed gantry or cable robot for polyculture beds (v2), drone + fixed cameras for sensing, animals for margins | Accepted 2026-10-09 | [form-factor-exploration](../design/form-factor-exploration.md); user confirmed daily mowing, solar, animals and a test plot are all welcome |

| D28 | 2026-10-09 | **Solar roof** (2 × ~110 W semi-flexible panels on a 30x30 roof frame) with an MPPT charger; **36 V 10–15 Ah pack; no charging dock.** GNSS antenna and e-stop move onto the roof's rear rail. | Accepted | ~500–740 Wh/day supply vs ~250 Wh/day typical need; removes phase 2's hardest item |
